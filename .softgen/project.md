## Vision
A white-label digital vehicle maintenance platform for independent workshops, dealerships, and fleet operators. Replaces paper service books with a secure, shareable digital service card accessed via QR code.

## Features
- Role-based auth (admin / owner)
- Vehicle registry with photos, documents, service history
- Digital service card with QR code
- Maintenance schedule by time and mileage
- Automatic email reminders
- Admin dashboard with analytics
- White-label branding
- PDF reports
